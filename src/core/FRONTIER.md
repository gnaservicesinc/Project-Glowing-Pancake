# Conditional futures and independent assessments

M7-01 adds the Frontier room to the century scenarios. The bundled frontier
and plateau campaigns run from 2014-01-01 through the close of 2113-12-31.
Their future branches open after the authored reference period. A separate
seeded stream chooses progress, energy, social adaptation, cooperation and
research conditions. The seed supplies conditions and a capability ceiling.
Actual paid research, licensed data, finite training and independent work
determine whether a deployed system qualifies.

These are fictional operational definitions. The accelerating branch supports
both advanced fabric and distributed compute. The uneven branch has a feasible
distributed route and a lower ceiling. The energy plateau permits game AGI and
commercial progress with a ceiling that excludes game ASI. Setup names and
assumptions disclose the difference. Future data, research, recipes and hardware
have typed condition gates checked by the ordinary Lab and Operations commands.

## Independent deployed evidence

Register a named system with one to four distinct deployed models and services.
Each member must have completed independent Lab evaluation and an operating
service. At least one member uses an advanced recipe. Registration pins exact
deployment versions. A replacement needs a new system registration.

| Qualification | Conservative independent evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Game AGI | All six domains at least 75, held-out transfer at least 70, reliability at least 85 and autonomous workflows at least 80 |
| Game ASI | All six domains at least 90, novel science and engineering at least 95, and three independently replicated improvements |

Three different held-out panels must observe the deployed system in separate
finite trials. The last observation must be at least 90 days after the first.
The third trial may be commissioned early enough to finish exactly at that
boundary. Conservative scores use each panel's margin and the minimum observed
lower bound across the panels. Paid fees, staff, physical work and actual useful
deliveries during each interval are required. A stopped service or insufficient
delivery invalidates its result.

Cooperating members can cover different domains. Transfer, reliability,
autonomous workflows and control inherit the weakest member. The authored
integration loss applies to all scores. Trial fees, fixed work and minimum
staff scale with the member count.

An advanced result then needs three distinct underlying research topics,
prototype experiments and independently reviewed replications. Each replication
uses a physical compute source different from its prototype. Three qualifying
replications must span at least 180 days. Same-source reruns cannot certify
independence. Control is displayed alongside capability and remains separate
from qualification. This module supplies dated outcomes to M7-03's objective
accounting.

## Bounded delegation

Frontier offers an ordered research queue of up to eight topics or a standing
track for one explicitly repeatable topic. Every start rechecks prerequisites,
conditions, the topic's completed experiment limit, staff, spare compute,
unreserved cash, the authorized total and cash floor. The player specifies the
minimum interval and maximum starts. Lab also exposes remaining repeat
experiments. Publications retain one completed result identity.

A mature refresh policy names an existing private service, a candidate recipe,
licensed commercial data, an independent evaluation plan and finite training
work. Its method-review research must be recent at both start and rollout.
Each candidate uses spare capacity while the incumbent continues serving.
The independent result must preserve previously covered evidence and improve
at least one covered domain by the authorized amount. Rollout rechecks current
rights, prerequisites, law, standards, resources and exact deployment identity.
It retains the service's channel, region, human review and compute source.

The policy waits with a visible reason when its next start cannot proceed.
Cancelling research stops future starts. Already funded Lab work retains its
normal project controls. Cancelling refresh stops an active candidate and
retains the incumbent. A manual replacement invalidates the pending refresh.
Each policy permits at most 12 starts, each type has at most eight active
policies and 64 saved authorizations, and the evidence register holds at most
64 systems and 512 trials. Every experiment consumes finite resources.

## Ownership, authoring and persistence

`frontier_catalog.cpp` validates authored conditions, metric profiles, technology
links and feasible recipe routes for each advertised ASI path. `frontier.cpp`
owns registration, quotes, funded trials, observations and qualification.
`frontier_automation.cpp` owns research and refresh authorizations. Runtime
supplies shared regional launch guards and prepared-morning ordering. Rivals
use the same Lab commands, trial quotes and qualification rules, with separate
funds and physical supply. Office reports their independently recorded outcomes.

The typed `frontier_research` catalog and optional scenario reference are
available in Studio's world and catalog forms. Conditions and metric axes have
fixed named lengths, and technology references use the selected scenario's
catalogs. The shared snapshot writer records finalized definitions and versions.
The collection byte budget is two MiB with existing nesting and event bounds.

Older content and saves omit the optional Frontier state. Single-run research
omits its default repeat limit in canonical data. Current saves retain policies,
pinned deployments, paid trial projects and results. Deep validation recomputes
conditions, costs, timing, work and evidence against the original records and
refuses forged scores.

## Focused Mac checks

`runtime.frontier` exercises paid player ASI routes in both future branches,
plateau AGI, a competing institution's ASI route, the exact 90/180-day boundaries,
cooperating-system costs and failure, cancelled work, queues, repeat experiments,
refresh refusal and rollout, and exact save validation. The native Frontier
walkthrough uses a deliberately late opening with the same authored terms and
an actual trained, evaluated and deployed model. At 100% and 200% text it drives
registration, paid assessment, queue and refresh review/cancellation, and exact
save reopening through Qt Quick controls on Metal.

An explicit scratch fixture can be prepared with
`pgp-frontier-test --smoke-fixture /path/to/new-scratch-directory`.
Run `pgp-game --smoke-test --frontier-smoke` with its `--content` collection,
`--scenario scenario.century_frontier` and matching `--portable-root`.
M7-02 adds succession, M7-03 adds the six objective endings, and M8 retains
century balance, endurance and full campaign acceptance.
