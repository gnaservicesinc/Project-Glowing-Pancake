# First-playable laboratory

`lab.hpp` owns delivered data licenses, research attempts, checkpoint lineage and
evaluation evidence. Scenarios supply a validated catalog; the shared compiler
hash includes every numeric input. The first-playable catalog in
`data/runtime/first-playable.json` has nine fictional data offers, six research
nodes, six training recipes, four evaluation plans and two synthetic-data
recipes. The data licenses apply to game
models. The game simulates training and evaluation offline.

All mutations use revision-checked `Simulation` commands and the existing
accounts/capacity transaction. Data and methodology costs are paid once per
accepted purchase/attempt, with no refund on cancellation. Training pays no
second compute charge for the leased capacity. Research requires both the
authored minimum experiment work and enough allocated experiment time for its
staff-days. A cancelled research attempt can restart with a new paid attempt.
Serving retains the capacity system's existing priority during shortages.

An authored first-experiment setback can settle the work without granting the
research result. The safeguard is checked against knowledge at the start.
The seeded sample is independent of evaluation draws and cancelled attempts.
A settled setback retains a finding and a bounded discount on the corrected
experiment. The retry still pays its fee and meets its staff-day floor.

An evaluation can explicitly reserve one onboarded specialist. Their authored
expertise reduces required experiment work, while the measurement formula,
coverage and cash fee retain the selected plan's terms. The specialist counts
within the entered staff allocation and can support one unfinished evaluation.
Paused tests keep that named assignment. Completion or cancellation releases
the specialist for a later test.

Training implements the simulation specification's single-round effective-data
and complementary saturation formula, in total milli-CU and basis points. A
model stores actual accumulated work, the recipe and a sorted unique data
mixture. Cloning a stopped checkpoint preserves exactly those resources and
capability. Continuing adds only new actual work. Continuation retains its
recipe/mixture; using new data requires a fresh candidate in this slice, so old
compute cannot instantly learn newly purchased data.

The Lab uses six axes in this order: documents, scanned tables, software and
reasoning, science and industry, social interaction, and autonomous
workflows. Recipes can support a subset. The sequence model uses specialized
code or dialogue data. The efficient recipe uses experiment and simulation
data. The image-feature recipe can learn from supervised robotics traces.
The introductory recipes and two-domain tests retain their original scopes
and the introductory campaign rubric scores those two domains.

Previews return time, license cost, bottlenecks and uncertain intervals, without
reading/drawing RNG. They assume full requested capacity; real outages delay
actual work. The immutable report omits true capability and stream state.
Evaluation requires a stopped model, methodology prerequisites, staff, compute
and payment. On completion it draws the saved research stream, retaining the
draw boundary for exact validation. Independent tests report modeled uncertainty;
overlapping headline tests can be biased by contamination. Neither raises true
capability. Findings name only covered subgroups and suggest targeted retraining,
human review or narrower promises. M3-02 now wires those responses into authoritative deployment and the player interface.

Choose the independent capability suite in Lab Evaluate to qualify the
specialist products. The coding API requires software evidence, the research
toolkit requires science evidence, the social companion requires interaction
evidence, and the robotics agent requires workflow evidence. The release
preview lists each threshold. Each additional axis keeps its own data fit,
training bottleneck and measured interval.

Saves retain all licenses/payments, research attempts, actual work, lineage,
evidence and RNG. Strict loading reconciles the authored catalog, actual capacity,
payments, prerequisite timing, setback outcome and retry terms, model formula
and every evaluation draw. Short
two-axis profiles retain their encoding and archived content retains its
identity. Normal save opening still requires the campaign's content generation.
History/draw replay is confined to load/save validation, not daily advancement.

Run `build/release/bin/pgp-headless --lab-demo data/runtime/first-playable.json`
for a baseline finding and targeted recovery. `runtime.lab-integration` covers a
hand-calculated arithmetic case, malformed data/saves, paid unlocks/retries,
resource/payment failures, cancellation, outage recovery, clone/mixture exploits,
untested subgroups, narrower audit uncertainty and matched save continuation.


## Commission a corpus or collect opted-in feedback

Lab Data lists paid collection agreements with provenance, coverage, volume,
and use rights. Review the fee, staff, compute and earliest delivery before
starting. Pausing releases the allocation and keeps the work already completed.
Cancelling delivers no corpus and keeps the fee spent.

A commission delivers the agreed volume once the job completes. Feedback uses
the named service and customer group, including settled signed and
discretionary work during the consent window. That window starts on the
acceptance day and keeps its end date during pauses. The reviewed volume is
the opted-in share of settled deliveries, rounded down and capped by the
agreement. A completed empty window can be retried as a new paid job.
Feedback grants exclude redistribution of trained weights.

Both paths produce an immutable training input on the morning after completion.
Saved jobs retain the agreement, capacity project, settlement volume and
result. Loading reconciles the fee, work, consent dates and actual deliveries
before accepting the corpus. Deployment uses the same rights lookup for
purchased, collected and generated data.

## Generate verified training data

Lab Data can commission a finite document or workflow generation job. Choose
an immutable trained teacher, its completed independent report, a separately
licensed verification source and paid compute/staff. The verification source
must be absent from the teacher's entire training lineage. Research and the
report must have finished before the job starts. The quote shows the one-time
fee, actual generation/filtering work, earliest completion, uncertain retained
yield, remaining source diversity and inherited commercial/distribution rights.

`synthetic_data.cpp` owns generation and validation. The job reserves a real
`data_generation` capacity project; it can pause, resume or cancel. Spent work
and fees stay spent. Completed verification exposes an immutable dataset for
a fresh training mixture on the next morning. Pending or cancelled jobs supply
no training data. Existing models retain their original mixture and work.

Yield depends on frozen teacher capability, verification quality and authored
diversity. Public estimates use the teacher's measured interval. Coverage stays
within both the independent report and verification source. Rights intersect
all teacher inputs and the verification license; contamination also follows the
inputs. One shared source/domain allowance limits repeated jobs. Cloning a
teacher cannot create a new diversity allowance. Cancelled jobs permit a new
paid attempt, while completed output retains its reservation.

Saves retain the teacher, report, verification source, accepted date, attempted
units, project and completed output. Loading checks their dates, actual work,
unique fee and derived output. Authored catalogs stay immutable. Empty synthesis
fields remain omitted so older archived content keeps its original hash.

## Qualified deployment

`DeployModel` owns the service transaction: select an authored scope, stopped
checkpoint, completed independent evidence and source. Evidence must measure
that exact checkpoint's consumed work; its lower bounds must meet each promised
covered domain. All inherited data must permit commercial use. Training previews,
headline tests and untested domains cannot qualify a promise.

`ServiceDefinition` carries task requirements and optional review credits,
throughput, staff/support costs and customer preferences. Review changes service
qualification, never the model/evidence score. Serving uses the selected recipe's
actual inference cost and shares finite source/staff reservations. Setup refuses
atomically when evidence, rights, resources or an existing customer promise fail.
The baseline runner uses this same command.

Replacing a service cancels its prior serving/support profile and reserves a new
one in one transaction, retaining product identity, contract terms, earlier
capacity bindings, delivery and invoices. Ordinary cancellation requires clearing
active/pending customer commitments first. Full save validation reconciles the
authored catalog, exact evidence, dated serving profiles and support obligations;
none of that history is replayed inside the daily advancement path.

`StageServiceRollout` qualifies a candidate through the same evidence and rights
checks, then reserves a smaller dated serving project. It uses the live product's
territory, quote and demand. Each buyer's settled discretionary work can route up
to the chosen share to the candidate if its actual resources and eligibility allow
it. Signed work always routes to production. The two workloads reconcile to one
customer charge. Customer-hosted candidates keep the current hardware footprint.
Support is prorated to the candidate's daily task cap and ends with the trial.

`FinishServiceRollout` discards the candidate or promotes it after actual trial
delivery. Promotion reserves a full production profile and rechecks signed terms
and current launch rules. At the deadline the trial reservation ends and the
decision stays available. `RollbackService` records a new production binding to
the previous distinct model, evidence and review configuration on its retained
source, subject to the same qualification and resource checks. Release history,
settled work, invoices and signed terms remain available after either decision.
An active geographic replica must be stopped before staging or restoring a release.

`CommitServiceCompatibility` publishes a 30–1,096 day window against a qualified
production binding. Updates, rollback and trial candidates preserve its daily
task capacity, lower bounds in the promised domains after review credit, paid
review where offered, and buyer hardware requirements. Hosted source changes
can still qualify. Installed integrations add a preference weighted by their
existing share and switching cost within ordinary task, price and budget limits.

`AnnounceServiceRetirement` publishes a binding date after all existing contract
and compatibility terms. Resolve any pending trial and stop an active geographic
replica before publishing. New signed terms and trials must fit before retirement.
While the notice is active, discretionary adoption caps at the latest installed
share. On the retirement morning the game closes any remaining trial decision,
releases serving resources and ends the scheduled support term. It retains the
release and billing history. Cash scenarios stop sales on the same date.

`runtime.deployment` covers qualification, commercial rights, paid review,
resource refusal/rollback, contract protection, replacement, clones, forged
history and exact saves. `bridge.management` checks player options, amount
parsing, preview purity, public evidence/control terms and stable selection.
