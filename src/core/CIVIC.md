# US institutions, elections and policy

Open **Civic** to see the governing blocs, upcoming dates and public docket.
Select a state to inspect its executive, assembly and local proposals. The
docket shows the votes already cast, the next scheduled decision, the rule's
jurisdiction and its actual effective and expiration dates.

While a hearing or chamber submission window is open, a company can disclose
one eligible independent evaluation or connected site record. An advocacy
submission pays the quoted expense from unreserved cash. Up to three paid
submissions per docket contribute the authored influence divided by one, two,
then three. Choose support or opposition before reviewing the expense. The
payment, evidence identity, position and effect on subsequent scores appear
on the public record. All cash amounts use constant-2014 dollars.

## Calendar and vote rules

- The fictional coalitions have positions on seven issues. State profiles
  define preferences, priority issue, sector exposure and a staggered
  executive cycle. District residents participate through their modeled local
  assembly and presidential electors.
- The ballot occurs on the Tuesday after the first Monday in November every
  two years from 2016. It apportions 435 House seats and local assemblies by
  largest remainder. One of three Senate classes is chosen by state plurality
  on each ballot. The other two classes retain their seats. The presidency
  uses a plurality of 538 modeled electors every four years. Chambers seat on
  January 3 and executives on January 20 of the following year.
- State ballot weights combine authored preferences, issue positions, bounded
  coalition campaign effort and a draw from the saved politics stream. The
  incumbent gets a bounded adjustment from the previous month's settled
  domestic service uptake and the recent executive decision record. Votes on
  a public docket use the institutions seated on that date. The 15-seat
  committee and each chamber require a strict majority. A state docket uses
  its assembly and governor. Federal dockets require both chambers and the
  president. A docket with a procedural review uses its disclosed vote floor.
- Each jurisdiction has one agency implementation slot. Approval reserves an
  implementation date after any queued work. The completed rule takes effect
  on that morning and expires at its exclusive term boundary. **Advance to
  next civic event** uses the same saved schedule as normal play.

## Eligibility and persistence

California and Washington reserve grid capacity under state permit rules.
New site requests above a rule's limit can use a smaller reservation or
another jurisdiction. Earlier approved requests retain their capacity and
dates. Federal launch rules apply to named channels offered to US customers.
They can require an independently evaluated lower bound in a specified domain.
The same check applies to player and rival launches. Existing deployments
retain their commitments. The catalog can exempt a funded human-review scope.

The core applies effective rules during the prepared morning. Dated decisions,
disclosures, policy terms, ballots and pending seating dates survive saves.
Reload validation replays civic boundaries and checks expense postings, evidence
and election stream transitions. The runtime also reconciles election inputs
with the prior settled US market review and validates historic launches and
permits against the rules in force at the time. News flows use the committed
election and implementation facts. Civic spending affects subsequent docket
scores and supplies no election draw or direct welfare change.

The bundled content is fictional. Studio exposes the US institutions catalog
and the linked scenario's policy sections through **Scenario and world**.
