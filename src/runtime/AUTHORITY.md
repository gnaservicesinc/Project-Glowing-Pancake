# Institutions and public commitments

The Institutions room shows the current soft and hard authority of eight
regions, organized resistance, and the population and output covered by
qualifying regions. Open **Public record** to disclose a completed independent
evaluation or promise a dated publication or public delivery. Open
**Disputes** to see the cause, response date, available actions and outcome.

Public delivery promises count actual settled tasks from the named service and
region after the pledge. Publication requires the completed research to be
published from Lab. A missed pledge opens a concern and then a formal
recommendation. Citing independent testimony allows one paid negotiation during
the formal window. It commits existing staff and compute through the extended
deadline. Actual publication or delivery resolves the case and releases the
review team. An unanswered case is disclosed and the failed promise is closed.
Acknowledging a notice keeps its response date.

Private publication disputes follow the named executive's appointment terms.
One funded formal review extends the response window. Publishing within that
window preserves the appointment. If it expires, the executive leaves under
their agreed vesting terms and the documented dispute enters the public record.
Testimony, kept and broken commitments, executive disclosures and coercive
actions affect the six audiences separately. The effects fade over the
authored memory window and appear alongside the effects of actual delivery.

Each agreement names the regional issuer, service, evidence, decision rights,
fees, payroll, compute source and expiration. The company pays and hires a
contract team at application. The decision checks actual administrative work
and public delivery during commissioning. A standards seat requires a
recorded regional forum ballot. Critical infrastructure oversight requires a
commissioned company-owned US site and operating source. Delegation requires
a fulfilled regional public-service promise. Soft agreements also require
regional consent.

An operating agreement can set or withdraw a binding regional evidence floor
for public procurement. It applies to all suppliers serving those buyers.
Blocked signed deliveries retain their credits and penalties. Regular reviews
use settled staff and compute work, delivered public tasks and the service's
current independent evidence. A shortfall opens a response case. Withdrawal,
expiry, denial and revocation end the corresponding paid team and rights.

The abstract hard authority path commits a paid institution and creates
opposition. Resistance grows during operation, carries across applications and
reduces effective control. Fulfilling a regional public delivery pledge can
reduce opposition at a subsequent monthly review. An unanswered resistance
case can end the agreement and suspend company sales in that region for the
authored sanction period.

The saved month-end record keeps each region's soft authority, hard authority
and resistance. It records separate population and output coverage for regions
with at least 60 out of 100 in either authority measure. These records supply
the later Domination objective. Paid media and ordinary sales do not produce
governing rights.
