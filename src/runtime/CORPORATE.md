# Corporate agreements, people and retained control

M4-03 extends the ordinary fixed financing/specialist offers with authored
counteroffers, an explicit board, named executive engagements and full-ownership
startup acquisitions. These optional rules live in the scenario's management
catalog. The introductory campaign keeps its prior small rules; the four full
competition presets supply the corporate catalog. Existing heavyweight funded
publication/pilot agreements remain in [HEAVYWEIGHTS.md](HEAVYWEIGHTS.md).

## Negotiated financing and board rights

Counteroffers in one `negotiation` group are alternatives: only one can be
pending or accepted. A proposal creates a dated board motion and transfers no
cash. The player votes their seats; other seats apply their disclosed minimum
cash after the action and maximum dilution. A strict majority decides. The
actual vote records its ledger boundary, seats and positions. Ignored motions
expire without accepting terms. Accepted financing pays once through the normal
accounts, with conserving Hamilton dilution or an actual interest-bearing debt.
Seats are separate rights; dilution alone cannot create or remove them.

The stock catalog offers smaller/larger independent equity terms, a loan with a
cash covenant and strategic research-partnership equity with a representative.
These negotiated terms are private; the pressure model continues to count only
financing explicitly marked for public announcement. Cash remains constant-2014
cents. Proposals and successful investments are distinct actions.

A cash-floor breach has a private concern, formal recommendation after seven
days and a thirty-day response deadline. Restore the actual cash floor or repay
the loan to cure it. A later breach starts a new episode. The disclosed one-time
paid waiver permanently ends that covenant, preserving interest and principal.
An unresolved breach accelerates the existing maturity to no later than thirty
days after the warned deadline. No debt, expense or insolvency exposure is erased.
Unread concerns, formal recommendations and outcomes stop advancement; actual
board/financial deadlines also stop the external fast-forward driver.

## Executives and the one reserved pool

Candidates have fictional names, key roles, expertise/risk descriptors,
priorities and explicit cash-only or hybrid compensation. Both alternatives pay
the signing cost and agreed recurring salary and add one real staff member.
The descriptors grant no instant capability improvement. A person accepts one
engagement; duplicate appointments and rehire/grant collection fail atomically.

An unvested grant is reserved *inside* the existing fully diluted option pool.
It is not another cap-table holding. After the cliff, daily preparation computes
cumulative earned entitlement and transfers only its new increment into a
beneficiary holding. Repeated preparation cannot vest twice. External equity
dilutes that holding and the unvested claim once; integer rounding never revokes
already earned ownership. Full vesting releases the remaining claim. Expanding
the pool requires a separately approved motion, disclosed pro-rata dilution and
stable residual allocation, still totaling 10,000 basis points.

A promised publication names actual research and a finite term. Completed but
unpublished research does not satisfy it. An unmet promise starts the concern /
recommendation / response path; an actual publication resolves it. On an
unresolved deadline, departure ends future salary and records a dated staff
release. Optional active work stops; past work/costs and customer promises stay.
Only the unvested claim returns to the pool; vested ownership remains.

## Acquisitions, subsidiary cash and group reports

Explicit due diligence quotes an entered, operating independent startup using
its actual net book assets plus the authored continuity premium. The seller
offers all cash or half cash plus equity valued from funded book value; the
rounded equity counteroffer must fit the authored ceiling. Review freezes the
observed revision. The board must approve, and buyer cash and seller control /
ownership are checked again at commit. No simple acquisition can bypass an
outstanding target executive grant or an already completed control transfer.

The buyer pays the recorded sellers, in exact conserving cash/equity portions
weighted by their outstanding holdings. The unallocated target pool dissolves.
Parent equity consideration is a noncash investment asset, not revenue or cash.
The target becomes a wholly owned operating subsidiary. Every license, model,
qualified product, staff record, invoice, debt, contract and bill remains in its
original simulation. Its paid planner continues; new external subsidiary
financing is not authorized. Acquisition does not itself cure insolvency or make
models/products compatible with another company.

The player can fund a subsidiary from actual unreserved parent cash. A dividend
is bounded by undistributed operating profit earned **after acquisition**, actual
cash, the authored reserve floor and forecast obligations over the reserve
period (stock rules: $100,000 and thirty days). Capital injections cannot be
recycled as operating profit. Both transfer legs commit in one world transaction
with receipts and exactly one world revision increment. Group cash conserves;
consolidated book net assets eliminate the parent's acquisition/funding assets,
and group operating profit excludes pre-acquisition earnings and internal
dividend income. Subsidiary closure retains its actual obligations; a closed
company cannot be revived by a fabricated transfer.

## State, validation and integration

Core owns compensation, grants, board decisions, covenant/promise responses,
capital history and shared account commands. Runtime owns bilateral acquisition
and group-transfer authorization. Session owns recovery saves/deadline halts;
the serialized worker carries immutable snapshots to persistent GUI models.
Office → Board and people exposes the decisions, actual votes, equity, causes,
deadlines, due diligence and subsidiary balances. Dialogs retain their selected
terms and scroll at 200% text; no QML callback mutates authoritative money.

Restore replays capital movements from the scenario's opening cap table,
reconciles paid staffing/payroll and grant schedules, checks board votes against
actual cash/seats and accepted counteroffers, and validates dispute evidence and
maturities. Acquisitions reconcile original seller ownership, due-diligence
posting boundaries, consideration and retained control. Group transfers reconcile
both ledger legs and earned-profit/cash limits, using one posting index per
company rather than replaying full history for each payment.

Bounds are eight candidates/finance terms, 128 motions, 32 dispute episodes,
8,192 capital events, 32 acquisition records and 2,048 transfers per acquisition;
all are explicit save limits. The same optional typed content/save extension
preserves legacy scenarios. Development saves still require the exact finalized
content identity. `runtime.governance` covers refusal, vesting/dilution, causes,
response windows, paid waivers, ownership transfers, group conservation and exact
reload. The explicit `--competition-smoke` adds the actual corporate QML path.
Century balance and objective accounting windows retain their later milestone
work. Century charters now provide [succession and the retained founder trust](../core/SUCCESSION.md),
with reviewed handovers in Leadership and typed charter fields in Studio. Human studies and platform /
release qualification remain deferred under the user's instruction.
