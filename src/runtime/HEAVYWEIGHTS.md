# Heavyweight response programs and funded agreements

M4-02 adds a saved four-state response to planned heavyweight profiles. The
original introductory scenario retains its bounded rival routine. A profile
with `response` uses that policy and sets the old deployment-trigger and
warning fields to zero; these alternative trigger systems cannot overlap.

## Public evidence and pressure

Each program reviews an authored interval of last-settled market history. Its
four capped integer components are revenue substitution, demonstrated customer
capability, adoption and publicly disclosed financed expansion. Substitution
values actual non-heavyweight customer deliveries at their public posted prices,
normalizes that estimate to a 30-day month and
compares them with the published incumbent revenue-at-risk baseline. Capability
counts distinct services that delivered actual customer work; adoption divides
that work by the public demand signal for the one shared customer pool. Private
contract prices and signed-demand floors are excluded. Financing counts only accepted
catalog offers explicitly marked `public_announcement` within six review
periods. Private equity commands, undisclosed offers, balances, model settings,
future draws and agreement fees do not contribute.

The weighted terms use checked arithmetic and floor rounding. Policy weights,
targets, observations, thresholds, next review and current public state are
shown in Office → Rivals. The baseline is a pressure-model input; actual program
accounts contain their single capital allocation and earned customer receipts.
Other startups can create pressure even when the player has not launched.
Public briefings are separate from future paid estimates of private activity.

## Watch, mobilize, compete, consolidate

Two reviews at or above the entry threshold announce a warning of at least
90 days. Two reviews at or below the lower exit threshold withdraw that warning
or move an active response into consolidation. Intermediate scores preserve
the current response instead of causing monthly flicker. Pressure can fall
while the visible warning is still running; all past notices remain recorded.

At the announced boundary, mobilization opens one finite program ledger. It
pays for an actual specialist and fixed-term compute order through the same core
commands used by the player. Payroll and lease charges accrue during delivery.
All capacity remains ineffective until the authored delivery lead ends. Only
then does the ordinary budgeted weekly planner start data/research/training and
independent evaluation. Entering `compete` supplies no trained model or service.
This program has no financing or catch-up catalog and receives no second
capital allocation.

Consolidation suspends new strategic work, cancels unanswered offers and honors
accepted funded obligations. Once those obligations resolve, `WithdrawOperations`
cancels active work, releases staff, ends ongoing payroll/support and disables
capacity. Dated staff departures preserve prior assignments and costs. Actual
fixed-term supplier bills keep their original expiry; withdrawal provides no
refund or bill erasure. After the cooldown, the program is marked as withdrawn
in `watch`. Renewed pressure cannot create replacement money, staff or a new
program. Insolvent programs close through the existing real response window.
Long-run reentry/consolidation balance remains M8 work.

## Optional funded agreements

A competing heavyweight can offer one publication partnership and one customer
pilot evaluation, subject to its actual available cash. It reserves each fee
before offering terms. The standard policy offers $50,000 for a completed
publication and $30,000 after 20,000 additional document-service customer tasks.
These are fictional scenario terms in constant-2014 dollars. The response
window is 30 days and the fulfillment term is 90 days, bounded by the campaign's
last settlement morning. The exact fee and projected deadline are visible
before the keyboard-accessible acceptance dialog opens.

Acceptance pays no fee. A publication must be completed and published after
acceptance, before its deadline; existing research prerequisites and delayed
knowledge diffusion still apply. Pilot progress uses only the selected
service's actual additional customer deliveries after acceptance. This
nonexclusive evaluation creates no extra customer demand, invoices or useful
work. Each issuer pays once from its own reservation; the receiver's commercial
receipt equals the issuer's expense exactly. Repeated/stale claims fail
atomically. These agreements do not transfer equity or implement an acquisition;
negotiated ownership, mergers and board obligations belong to M4-03.

Declining or ignoring terms releases the unspent reservation. Unmet accepted
terms expire without a fee; past work remains paid. Actual counterparty closure
records failure and retains its frozen commitment. The player never pays an
upfront deposit into these agreements. Optional expiration notices and known
milestone controls show the recorded boundaries; no story response or offer is
automatically accepted.

## Commands, saves and checks

All cross-company responses run in the owning runtime transaction, through the
session's pre-commit recovery-save policy and serialized campaign worker.
Pressure inspection is pure. Saves retain public reviews, transitions, warning/
delivery/withdrawal dates, finite offer terms, reservations, work cursors,
responses and bilateral receipts. Deep restore recomputes public observations
and response decisions, verifies actual hires/leases and retained obligations,
rejects premature work and forged notices/fees, and reconciles offer progress
and both sides of payment. Named current fields coexist with the existing
lossless rival-history rows/string tables.

`runtime.heavyweights` covers warning/delivery reloads, public versus private
funding, disclosure/bound validation, both paid milestones, stale/repeated claims,
ignored/declined terms, hysteresis/withdrawal, retained fixed-term bills,
unaffordable mobilization, insolvency and no replacement subsidy. The explicit
`--competition-smoke` path also reviews the public briefing, declines a paper
offer, accepts and completes a funded pilot through actual QML controls, then
saves/reloads the full ending. Human playtests, author studies, platform packages
and release qualification remain deferred under the user's instruction.
