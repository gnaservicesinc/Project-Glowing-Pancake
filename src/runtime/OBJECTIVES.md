# Century objectives and postmortems

Choose a primary objective and any secondary distinctions before starting a
century campaign. The plateau explains why ASI Race is unavailable. Objectives
shows the rules, settled results and the first check that put a goal out of
reach. Changing the primary objective permanently marks the campaign custom,
even if you change it back. The settled dates and original deadline remain.

The default century settles its final day on **2113-12-31**. All accounting
uses constant 2014 cents. Only the primary ASI race can end in an early win.

| Objective | Settlement |
| --- | --- |
| World Monopoly | Sum actual useful tasks from January 2109 to December 2113. Require 85% globally and 60% in every region, with delivered work in all eight. A licensed or open deployment credits its publisher. The wholesale host gets no duplicate task. An acquired institution contributes to its parent only from the actual acquisition date. |
| World Domination | Each of the same 60 month-end checks requires authority of at least 60/100 in regions covering at least 80% of both population and output. The checks use settled institutional agreements and resistance. |
| World Utopia | All 120 month-end checks from January 2104 to December 2113 require global welfare of at least 90/85/80/80/75 in the five published indices and at least 70 in every index in every region. A scenario-ending catastrophe rules out victory. |
| Profit | Compare founder trust wealth. Equity is realizable cash and assets, less payables and debt, plus the charter multiple of positive final-year audited operating profit, floored at zero. |
| ASI Race | All independently validated institutions on the earliest date share first place. Frontier reports control and safety separately. |
| Simple Survival | The founding institution must operate with an active team, serviced liabilities or an acknowledged current workout, and paid useful work, metered paid wholesale work or funded research actually performed in the final year. |

The default valuation takes cash at face value, receivables at 80%, and
depreciated physical assets at 60%. The goodwill multiple is three, bounded
by the charter schema to at most five. Company cash appears inside equity.
Audit income comes from actual customer and external wholesale receipts;
debt, grants, internal dividends and one-off transfers cannot generate
goodwill. Subsidiary balances consolidate without the parent's investment
asset. The audit uses subsidiary operating results only after acquisition.
Beneficial ownership prices the retained founder stake and actual seller
consideration. Paid personal cash and liabilities are separate. Exact
wealth ties share first place.

Century regions can build durable gains through actual independently
qualified public delivery after the observation lag. The World room gives the
published coverage, quality, growth, decay and cap. The century catalogue
offers a separate local public toolkit for each region so one qualified model
can serve several places through independently installed editions. These
editions use real customer hardware. An approved investment or an
advertising campaign does not itself award welfare.

A failed mandatory month immediately pauses a selected Domination or Utopia
objective. Monopoly only becomes impossible when even the maximum remaining
customer demand, all assigned to your institution, cannot make the aggregate
pass. A weak month can recover. Acknowledge the review to continue toward
other scored goals, end immediately at the postmortem, or choose a custom
sandbox. Continuing keeps the loss and its original check date.

The postmortem also distinguishes a closed insolvency workout, loss of the
last directing right, a final effective federal order, and an authored
world-ending event. It retains the actual overdue ledger amounts and causal
history. A takeover proposal or a normal executive handover does not end
play. After institutional failure, Observer advances rivals and regional
outcomes while preserving the founding institution's final records.

Saves retain the chosen goals, settled checks, race ties, first breaches,
review dates and outcome. A sandbox keeps the original result and can play
past the scoring deadline. Longer century saves store the same regional
delivery and accounts journals in compact rows; restore still checks the
source receipts and preserves the full records. This is separate from the
100-year and 500-year endurance qualification.

The focused tests are `runtime.objectives` and `runtime.frontier`.
`pgp-objectives-test --terminal-replay` runs a purpose-built final-decade
fixture through the five non-race wins, a last-day authority loss, exact
reload and a day beyond the original deadline. It uses a strong opening
welfare baseline and a weighted regional mandate so the replay exercises
the rules without purporting to qualify century balance. The
`--smoke-fixture` and `pgp-game --objectives-smoke` path starts before a real
first-window check and exercises the keyboard, breach reload, postmortem
and both continuation controls.
