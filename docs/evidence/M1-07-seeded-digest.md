# M1-07 seeded incidents and weekly news

The same graph executor handles narrative branches and bounded incident reviews.
An incident samples at a scheduled or interval opening only after its guard
passes and its lifecycle permits a review. The saved markets and hazards
streams are independent of narrative and one another. The explicit
`no_event` outcome ends immediately and still counts toward the review limit.
Other results follow the existing validated graph, including delayed delivery
and required decisions. Authoring a routine incident uses the shared
[content schema](../../src/content/README.md).

The save retains each resolved sample, the selected branch, and stream cursors.
Validation checks the eligible date and sampled entry, enforces a single
sample per scoped definition/date, replays the draw on a temporary generator
and checks the saved stream continuation. It rejects a quiet activation with
effects or presentations. Format-1 saves with no system incident history keep
their prior shape. This batch does not qualify content migrations.

An independent integer oracle freezes seed 6. The markets stream samples a
quiet result on day 2 and a selected result on day 5. Hazards also selects a
scheduled result on day 5. Narrative draws independently on day 0. The fixture
checks no samples before or between due dates, false-guard suppression, the
review cap, a delay and required dialogue, and exact reload at each pending
boundary. It also rejects invalid sampling graph shapes and corrupted history.

The Inbox groups ordinary news by seven-day windows anchored at campaign
start. The projection retains every original presentation and source.
Each displayed digest part has up to eight entries and a stable week/part ID.
Unresolved choices and active liquidity deadlines remain individual rows and
sort above routine news, including in alphabetical order. Acknowledgment
clears the warning pause while its still-active deadline remains in this tier.
Content news keys can resolve through the local Qt translation context.
The development fallback is the readable flow name and any actual owning cause.
