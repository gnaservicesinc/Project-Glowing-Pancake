# E1A-03 flow impact inspection

The Studio runs its current working collection on a chosen scenario and
headquarters, using an explicit unsigned 64-bit seed or the scenario seed.
Authors can isolate the selected root or test the authored scenario roots.
The Qt-free probe owns a copied program, world and RNG. Advance-to-date yields
between days and stops at a required decision. The preview uses the game’s
effect executor, accounting, scheduler, save codec and presentation component.
Changing the working document discards its stale test state.

The inspector displays current trigger and continuous exit predicates
separately from conditions evaluated by the last real runtime operation.
It shows actual clause values, units, comparisons, duration samples, matched
entity IDs, execution paths, committed receipts, cash counterparty movement,
ledger postings, active modifier owners and nested continuation frames.
Candidate traces retain their rolled-back labels after a rejected operation.
Player-facing runtime traces keep their hidden-fact redaction.

The boundary action evaluates individual numeric literal clauses immediately
below, equal to and above their thresholds, at zero and with an absent binding.
Range and binding failures show errors. These fact-only clause probes carry
their own label. A separate synthetic cleanup operation runs on another copy,
reports its owner removal and preserves the natural test timeline.

## Verified Mac behavior

The optimized Apple arm64 build passed with Qt 6.12.0. The Studio executable
passes 180 library/form assertions and 43 copied-fixture assertions.
Five focused CTests pass for the Studio, flow execution, weighted branches,
durations and selectors.

- Two ordered cash effects expose exact values, resolved self target and
  balanced debit/credit direction. A rejected later transfer commits no
  receipt, posting or RNG change.
- Nested sequence frames wait two days, draw one seeded branch and suspend
  inside a reusable dialogue flow. Reload at the wait and at the choice
  produces the uninterrupted state, including the outer sibling and result.
- Two continuous owners enter once and survive an active tick. A scheduled
  real cash change makes one natural exit eligible. Its removal preserves
  the other owner. Synthetic cleanup leaves the natural state intact.
- Duration observations retain their first-sample and elapsed-day meanings
  through reload. Related capacity sources retain their actual member binding.
- Hidden variable values are visible to the author fixture and redacted from
  the default player trace.

The hidden Qt check loads the real library, accepts the maximum unsigned seed,
preserves an existing fixture after rejecting a fractional seed, advances its
date, reloads its checkpoint and exercises the shared dialogue/result card.
Undoing a document change invalidates the old fixture. The game’s explicit
Metal smoke passes at 1280×720 and 200% text with keyboard navigation, running
focus retention and exact save/load after sharing the presentation component.

The inspection UI uses a validated scenario from the working collection.
Boundary overrides cover numeric literal clauses. Broader screen-reader,
native-device and release qualification remain in their owning deferred tasks.
