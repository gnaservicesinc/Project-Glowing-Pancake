## Fresh evidence and remaining uncertainty

Read-only local checks on 2026-11-03 confirmed Qt `6.12.0`, no `lib/cmake/Qt6Multimedia`, and installed SBOMs for qtbase, qtdeclarative, qtdoc, qtimageformats, qtsvg, qttools and qttranslations. A CycloneDX JSON parsed successfully. These are SDK facts, not a deployed-game inventory or license clearance.

The default `clang++` resolves to `/usr/local/bin/clang++` (LLVM 23.1.3); `/usr/bin/clang++` reports Apple Clang 21.0.0. `xcrun --find clang++` reported a compiler under `Xcode-beta.app`, while `xcrun --show-sdk-path` reported an SDK under `Xcode.app`. M0 must establish one coherent selected compiler/SDK and inspect linkage rather than assume the review's proposed pairing is already sound. No compilation or SDK changes were performed.

Free space measured approximately **32.5 GiB**, superseding the review's transient 12 GB claim. The project still avoids heavy local SDK/build staging. Exact five-target Qt download availability, Actions execution, platform audio/graphics and native devices remain M0/qualification work; this review does not mark them done. The primary sources used to check technical assertions are listed in [research](RESEARCH.md#baseline-12-review-verification).
