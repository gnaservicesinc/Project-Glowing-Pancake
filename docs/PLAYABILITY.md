# Play the founder campaign

Choose **New Game**, complete the birth record and school scenes, then use the home computer to form a company or pursue an appointment. The family choice changes savings and available rooms. Review the agreement before committing. An employed CEO inherits the board and charter.

For a direct start, open **Playtest Environment → Custom playtest setup & start**. Set the launch date, biography, company finances, staff, equipment, research and data. Read the inline requirements before starting. The new company opens paused in its own playtest storage.

## From a research question to a product

1. Open **Devices & apps**, select a working laptop or workstation and open **Research**. Use the list or graph to choose a topic. The inspector explains the experiment, prerequisites, eligible data, staff and compute.
2. In **Data**, name a collection, select its sources and review the fee, staff, compute and rights before starting. Finished collections can earn license revenue when the company owns the IP in every contributing source. You can also buy a published dataset or license one from another company. Processing changes its properties while retaining the original source and rights. In Research, select the team and data, check the quote and start. Advance the clock to do the work.
3. In **Models**, choose a recipe and balance source weights against sample coverage. Train the model and evaluate it. Some research produces an early demonstration model that can seed later production training.
4. In **Products**, review evidence and commercial rights before deploying. In **Accounts**, select a spending category to inspect its ledger entries. Personal education debt belongs in **My life**.

Longitudinal sampling, hardware turnover and service drift studies show when the next observation window opens. Acquire or produce a qualifying dataset after the previous result, then return to the same topic. Each follow-up gives a smaller improvement to future models.

Each physical device remembers its open apps, drafts and research selection. Use **Desktop** to switch apps and **Pop out** to move a device to a separate window. Direct management is available from the main window. A failed or powered-off device keeps its workspace.

## Design activities

- **Software Studio** previews the interface as you change navigation, style, priorities and team. Review work and cost before starting development.
- **Models** shows how much of each dataset fits the training budget when you change sample weights.
- **Arrange headquarters** previews walking routes and affected desks as you move equipment. The infrastructure readout compares rack placement, power margin, heat and redundancy before a move. Keyboard coordinates and rotation use the same placement rules.
- **Headquarters → Branch design** lets you change the hardware, grid reservation, operator count, cooling method and state. Compare cost and design scores, save a plan, then review construction before spending company money.
