# Prologue art and pacing

The game uses original vector silhouettes with ink shadows, warm paper text
and one dominant accent per scene. Windows, horizon lines and pools of light
connect the scenes. The menu and room illustrations use the same color roles
from [GameInk.qml](../../qml/game/GameInk.qml).

| Scene | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| `hospital.svg` | Slate ward and a single bassinet. The supplied hospital photos grounded the window, curtain and wall rail. The adult bed is outside the frame. |
| `birth-certificate.svg` | Original parchment border, registry ornament and infant footprint. The live QML fields, signature and seal sit above it. |
| `street.svg` | Ochre bungalow street and a low morning sun. |
| `street-apartments.svg` | Blue apartment row for the tight-budget family background. |
| `street-hillside.svg` | Olive ridge and a long window wall for the wealthy background. |
| `library.svg` | Jade shadows, a CRT and the light at the end of the shelves. |
| `workshop.svg` | Plum dusk around the founder's borrowed desk. |
| `company.svg` | Amber window light on a corporation's term sheet. |
| `trust.svg` | Jade meeting room and open ledger for a nonprofit. |

The chosen family background sets the home art on subsequent beats. The legal
form sets the scene and text accent through signing. The narration and
available choices carry the meaning in words.

The prologue dissolves between scenes over 2.1 seconds. The opening gives the
title and both paragraphs separate entrances. Decisions reveal the heading
and narration before the options become active. Enter, Space or a click on
the scene reveals the current beat early. Reduced motion shows it immediately.

The prologue alternates existing excerpts from **Gedanklichkeit** and
**Atemübungen** by **Sascha Ende** (CC BY 4.0), at 60% of the selected music
volume. The cue fades when entering or leaving the prologue. The 28-second
excerpts retain their source credits in `assets/audio/CREDITS.json` and in-game
Credits.

The references informed the art direction and staging. The drawings and their
hashes are recorded in [PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json). The developers'
account of how life choices enter the game is available in [Torpor Games'
Suzerain prologue notes](https://www.indiedb.com/games/suzerain/features/prologue1).
